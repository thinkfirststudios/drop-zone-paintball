Can I bring my own paint?
Yes, if you're walking on with your own gear — $30 with unlimited air. “one of the few fields left that allows you to use your own paint if you are walking on with your own gear.”
Are air fills included?
Yes. Unlimited air fills are included in every package. Compressed air bottles are free to loan.
How do I get 200 paintballs instead of 100?
Reserve online. Walk-on is $31.48 either way — reserving online gets you 200 paintballs instead of 100.
Do I need a waiver?
