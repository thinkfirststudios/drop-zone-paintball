Low Impact

Stings a bit less.

Standard paintball: .68 cal

Low impact: a smaller, lighter .50 cal paintball with 1/3 the energy of a standard .68 — Drop Zone's published figure

  • Less mess
  • Lighter hit
  • Shorter range
  • Lighter gear and smaller air bottles
